    How it was made

    The drawings in the video are the vessel's actual general arrangement, deck plan and midship section, re-lit as holograms and scaled at 1:400 so every overlay sits on real frame positions.

    Every figure comes from her stability information manual, grain stability manual and cargo loading manual: hold volumes, lightship, hydrostatics and KN tables, volumetric heeling moments, the maximum allowable heeling moment tables, and the manual's 8-pour homogeneous loading sequence, scaled to corn.

    The calculation runs in code, the animation is rendered frame by frame from that data, and the narration is synthetic. The light-ballast and first-pour drafts match the manual's figures after correcting for fresh water.

    Illustrative only, not for operational use. Corn stowage factor of 48 ft³/LT assumed. Shear force and bending moment during loading are not shown.
